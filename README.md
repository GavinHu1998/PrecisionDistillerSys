## Overview

Precision Water Systems (PWS) designs and manufactures in-home and commercial water distillers. Distillation boils water and collects the steam, leaving contaminants behind in the boiling chamber, so you get clean, pure water every day.

PWS distillers are **CSA/UL certified**, tested for safety, performance, and/or energy efficiency for Canada and the USA. Units are built from durable stainless steel and made to order.

## What a PWS Distiller Removes

| Contaminant type | Examples | How it's removed |
|---|---|---|
| Inorganic minerals & salts | Calcium, magnesium, sodium, fluoride, nitrates, heavy metals (lead, arsenic) | They don't vaporize, so they stay behind as residue |
| Microorganisms | Bacteria, viruses, protozoa | Destroyed by the heat of boiling; not carried over in the vapour |
| Chemical contaminants | Most VOCs and industrial chemicals | Removed during distillation (activated carbon filtration also helps) |

## Quick Start

> Always follow the owner's manual supplied with your unit. The steps below are a general overview.

1. **Place** the distiller on a stable, level countertop near a cold water supply, a drain or drain line, and a grounded outlet of the correct voltage.
2. **Connect** the water inlet (and drain, if using the drain kit).
3. **Check** that the fan and power switches are off, then plug in the unit.
4. **Start** the unit by turning on the POWER and FAN switches. The distiller fills automatically, heats, and begins producing distilled water.
5. **Collect** the distilled water from the spout into a clean glass or stainless-steel container.
6. **Discard** the first batch of water from a brand-new unit, as recommended in the manual.

## Maintenance

Regular cleaning keeps output and water quality consistent.

- **Descale** the boiling chamber periodically with [PWS Descaler](https://www.precisionwatersystems.ca/products/pws-descaler). Mineral buildup reduces efficiency, and the frequency depends on your water hardness.
- **Replace the carbon filter media** (PWS Activated Coconut Charcoal) at the interval stated in the manual.
- **Inspect gaskets and seals** and replace worn parts (e.g. heater gasket #500100).
- **Heating elements** are replaceable if performance drops.
- **Wipe down** the exterior with a soft, damp cloth. Don't use abrasive cleaners on stainless steel.

## Safety Notes

- The boiling chamber, steam path, and exterior surfaces get **hot** during operation. Allow the unit to cool before servicing.
- Use only the correct voltage. The **220V model is for European markets only** and is not compatible with North American power plugs.
- Keep the unit on a stable surface and away from children.
- Unplug the unit before cleaning, descaling, or replacing parts.
